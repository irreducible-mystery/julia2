  1. THE ANALYTIC HEART — conditional gain is positive EVERYWHERE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    Observed A = 2^n. Posterior P(you hold the SMALL one | A):
    n = 0:  P(small) = 1        E[gain | switch] = +1
    n = 1:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 2    ] =  +0.2000  (= A/10)
    n = 2:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 4    ] =  +0.4000  (= A/10)
    n = 3:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 8    ] =  +0.8000  (= A/10)
    n = 4:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 16   ] =  +1.6000  (= A/10)
    n = 5:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 32   ] =  +3.2000  (= A/10)
    n = 6:  P(small) = 0.4000   E[gain | A = 64   ] =  +6.4000  (= A/10)
    ⇒ For EVERY possible observation, switching has expected gain A/10 > 0.
      By symmetry the other player computes the same. Both «should» switch.
      Both cannot be right. And yet no single conditional step is wrong.

  3. THE DIAGNOSIS — non-conglomerability, computed
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    Σ over observation-cells of P(A)·E[gain | A]  (partial sums):
      through n =  0:     0.16667
      through n =  1:     0.22222
      through n =  2:     0.29630
      through n =  5:     0.70233
      through n = 10:     2.95962
      through n = 20:    52.55614
      through n = 30:   933.27761
    → the by-observation sum DIVERGES to +∞,
      while the same sum organized by PAIRS is 0 by symmetry:
      Σ over pairs of p(n)·E[gain | pair n] = 0.0

    Two partitions of the SAME event space give different «totals»
    because E[amount] DIVERGES — the honest divergence certificate:
      E[amount] = Σ term(n),  term ratio term(n+1)/term(n) = 1.3333  = 4/3 > 1 ⇒ terms GROW
      partial sum through n = 10:           34.02
      partial sum through n = 30:        11197.83
      partial sum through n = 60:     62712509.78
      With E[amount] = ∞, E[gain]'s positive and negative parts are
      each infinite; their «difference» is an ∞ − ∞ ledger whose value
      depends on summation order — Riemann's rearrangement theorem
      is the engine under the paradox.

  4. RIEMANN REARRANGEMENT — the same series, any answer you like
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    rearranged to target  0.00000 → partial sum settles at  0.00002
    rearranged to target  1.00000 → partial sum settles at  0.99999
    rearranged to target  0.69315 → partial sum settles at  0.69314
    rearranged to target  3.14159 → partial sum settles at  3.14086
    A conditionally convergent ledger has no observer-independent total —
    «the value of switching» in Broome's game is exactly such a ledger.

  5. WHERE IT LANDS — what resolves, what remains
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
RESOLVED (theorem-level):
  • No paradox in any FINITE truncation: cap the pairs at n ≤ N and
    the top observation A = 2^(N+1) is CERTAINLY the large envelope —
    its guaranteed loss exactly cancels the whole interior advantage:
        N =  5:  interior advantage   +0.7699  boundary cell   -0.7699  total +1.11e-16
        N = 10:  interior advantage   +2.9942  boundary cell   -2.9942  total -4.44e-16
        N = 20:  interior advantage  +52.5667  boundary cell  -52.5667  total +7.11e-15
        The «paradox» is the boundary term surviving the N → ∞ limit
        as a ghost: it never appears at any finite A, yet its weight×size
        does not vanish.
  • The «always switch» ARGUMENT is invalid: it applies conglomerability
    to a non-conglomerable expectation. That step, and only that step,
    is the formal error.

NOT RESOLVED (still contested in the literature):
  • What should a rational agent DO facing a genuinely infinite-mean
    prior, observing A, with positive conditional gain at every A?
    Answers on offer — forbid such priors (finitism), abandon
    expectation as the decision criterion (bounded utility, medians),
    accept non-conglomerable «local» advice as rational — each gives
    up something. This is the same shape as the Sorites verdict:
    NO NEUTRAL RESOLUTION; you choose which intuition to sacrifice.

Field-note for this repo: this is the cleanest concrete case for the
proposed `incommensurable_priors` relation — the two partitions
(by-amount vs by-pair) are two projections π₁, π₂ of one microstate
space whose induced expectations DISAGREE, and no finer common
refinement restores agreement while the mean diverges. In Part 2's
language: the commutation diagram π∘F ≈ G∘π fails non-removably.
