=== 1. ¬ is literally 1-x: total on ALL numbers ===
  ¬0, ¬1:        ⟨ 1 0 ⟩
  ¬0.3:          0.7
  ¬2:            ¯1
  ¬¯5:           6
  Prolog's \\+ is partial (flounders); ¬ is total. No 'cannot be
  proven' exists here -- every input HAS a negation. The Closed
  World seam is gone... because there is no world, only numbers.

=== 2. The involution seam: when is ¬¬x = x? ===
  ¬¬0.3 = 0.30000000000000004
  ¬¬¯7  = ¯7
  CONFESSION (kept, per protocol): this file's first draft said
  'ALWAYS -- 1-(1-x)=x is algebra.' The machine refused: ¬¬0.3
  printed 0.30000000000000004. The involution holds in R and
  FAILS in float64 -- my claim was about the abstraction, the
  run was about the carrier. So BQN's double-negation law is
  EXACT on integers-in-float and dyadic rationals, approximate
  elsewhere: even the classical law you buy is horizon-graded.
  (Prolog's \\+ \\+ G =/= G is a PROOF gap; intuitionism's ~~G
  =/= G is a CONSTRUCTION gap; float ¬¬x =/= x is a CARRIER gap.)

=== 3. De Morgan seam: ∧∨ are ×(+-×) -- laws hold only on 0‿1 ===
  on Booleans: ¬(a∧b) ≡ (¬a)∨(¬b)?  1
  off-domain:  a=2, b=0.5:
    ¬2∧0.5     = 0
    (¬2)∨(¬0.5) = 0
  De Morgan BREAKS off 0‿1: ∧ is ×, ∨ is +-×, and the identities
  1-ab = (1-a)+(1-b)-(1-a)(1-b) hold iff a,b∈0‿1 (or fuzzy [0,1]:
  there they're Łukasiewicz-ish but ∧ is product t-norm, not min).
  The seam: BQN exports 'not' beyond logic, and the LAWS quietly
  become CONTINGENT identities true on a subdomain.

=== 4. The fixed-point seam (the Liar's home) ===
  ¬0.5 = 0.5
  Prolog: liar detonates Clark completion / goes UNDEFINED in WFS.
  BQN: x=¬x is an EQUATION, solution ½. The liar seam is not
  avoided -- it is RELOCATED into the codomain. Cost: ½ is not a
  truth value anyone asserted; it is where the algebra says the
  oscillation balances. Meaning was traded for solvability.

=== 5. The comparison seam: ¬ vs ≠ vs 'not equal to anything' ===
  (0.1+0.2)≠0.3?  1
  (and a meta-seam caught live: my first draft wrote 0.1+0.2≠0.3
   unparenthesized -- BQN's right-to-left parse made it 0.1+(0.2≠0.3)
   = 1.1. The NOTATION has seams too.)
  Absence-of-equality is IEEE ≠, a DIFFERENT negation than ¬, with

  representations). Prolog also had two (\\+ vs dif/2). Pattern:
  EVERY language that splits negation splits it along proof/value.
